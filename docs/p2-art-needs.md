# Art brief for campaign islands 1–16

The 16 campaign bakes use six painted biome kits. The table records the palette, props and intended landmark placement for each island. The road, gate mouths, flight path and numbered build plots in the corresponding layout are the placement reference. Keep tall silhouettes outside the plot and tower clearances. [Review the campaign bakes at 1x](art-themes-sheet.jpg).

| Island | Ground palette | Props | Landmark and placement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| i01 · Sunlit Meadow | Warm leaf green, pale buttercup yellow, honey-coloured dirt | Low wildflower drifts, split-rail fence ends and a few fruit trees | A whitewashed cottage and kitchen garden in the pocket below the eastern uphill bend. Leave the diagonal flight across the meadow readable. |
| i02 · Twin-Bridge Cay | Blue-green channel water, salt grass, wet umber mud | Mooring posts, rope coils, beached dinghies and timber dock ends | Two timber bridges cross the same narrow channel on the outward and returning roads. Put a small harbour shed at the southern landing. |
| i03 · Highland Citadel | Slate blue, snow-shadow violet, pine green, pale gravel | Snow-tipped firs, broken parapets and cairns | A courtyard keep inside the inward spiral. A waterfall can spill over the eastern margin beside the outer climb. |
| i04 · Emerald Stepping Stones | Emerald moss, jade ledges, turquoise crystal light | Short rope posts, hanging lanterns and crystal clusters | Raise the northern road onto a terrace reached by a rope viaduct. Keep the western cave mouth visible against the lower terrace. |
| i05 · Windmill Switchback | Wheat gold, amber stubble, russet orchard soil | Hay sheaves, low orchard rows and fence corners | Keep the mill between the western hairpins. Angle the field rows across the straight runs so players can distinguish each leg at a glance. |
| i06 · Crescent Lagoon | Pale coral sand, lagoon teal, seagrass green | Low palm fans, tide pools, shell banks and driftwood | A lighthouse overlooking the inner cove, with water inside the crescent and surf on the outer rim. Leave the eastern shoreline open around the west-facing cave. |
| i07 · Skybridge Gauntlet | Wind-worn grey stone, cool blue chasm, faded ochre turf | Snapped standards, low crenellations and bridge anchor blocks | Paired cliffside forts flank the chasm. Span it with stone on both the outward crossing and the lower return to the western gate. |
| i08 · Sunken Bastion | Peat brown, drowned olive, dark teal water, warm torchlight | Half-submerged masonry, reed clumps, torch brackets and broken coping | Flood the outer bailey and surround the inner gate with a moat. Use a short causeway on the final approach and a quieter water surface under the flyer circuit. |
| i09 · Grand Skyhold | Ivory marble, cloud blue, pale gold and clipped lawn green | Golden standards, low balustrades, carved finials and trimmed cypress | Put the crown hall at the end of the inner court. Use a marble causeway where the western road crosses to the citadel. |
| i10 · Confluence Terraces | Ochre terrace faces, fern green, pale river silt | Dry-stone risers, drainage channels, low willow stools and stone culverts | Mark the junction with a split irrigation channel. The northern switchback and long western causeway should both be visible from the junction. |
| i11 · Galewatch Spires | Blue-grey shale, cold cloud shadow, silver grass | Wind vanes, torn pennants, shear faces and low mooring rings | Raise two signal spires beside the chasm. Keep the east-to-west air crossing clear so players can follow falling haulers to the road below. |
| i12 · Lanternfall Hollow | Dusk violet, plum earth, blue moss and pools of amber light | Cropped hedgerows, lantern hooks, low mushrooms and dark hazel stumps | Set a lantern shrine beside the final bend. Use pale grass for the sheltered central plots and hedge shadows for the exposed switchbacks. |
| i13 · Stormcrown Citadel | Storm blue, wet slate, tarnished silver and pale lightning light | Broken spires, lightning rods, split masonry and wind-bent trees | Place the storm altar in the spiral’s inner court. Keep a gap in the parapet where the northern war-road opens onto the inner ring. |
| i14 · Amberstone Mesa | Baked amber, rust-red wash, buff sandstone and sage | Low agave, shale fans, dry creek stones and eroded pillars | Set a sandstone waystone beside the three-way plaza. Give the fast eastern wash a pale gravel bed distinct from the western dune steps and northern scarp. |
| i15 · Moonwell Crossing | Moonlit indigo, silver loam, blue-green reeds | Low reed fans, crescent stones, pale water lilies and small boundary posts | Place the well just off the self-crossing. Continue the same paving across it so players can see the same raider pass twice. |
| i16 · Emberglass Sanctum | Obsidian black, ember red, smoky umber and copper | Low glass shards, clinker banks, cooling seams and bronze braziers | Line the upper and lower ring walls with dark glass. Put the furnace sanctuary at the inner end of the causeway and leave the eastern breach wide enough to read as the short third lane. |
