# Art brief for campaign islands 1–8

The playable bakes use the existing meadow kit. Each row describes the next painted pass. The road, gate mouths, flight path and numbered build plots in the corresponding layout are the placement reference. Keep tall silhouettes outside the plot and tower clearances.

| Island | Ground palette | Props | Landmark and placement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| i01 · Sunlit Meadow | Warm leaf green, pale buttercup yellow, honey-coloured dirt | Low wildflower drifts, split-rail fence ends and a few fruit trees | A whitewashed cottage and kitchen garden in the pocket below the eastern uphill bend. Leave the diagonal flight across the meadow readable. |
| i02 · Twin-Bridge Cay | Blue-green channel water, salt grass, wet umber mud | Mooring posts, rope coils, beached dinghies and timber dock ends | Two timber bridges cross the same narrow channel on the outward and returning roads. Put a small harbour shed at the southern landing. |
| i03 · Highland Citadel | Slate blue, snow-shadow violet, pine green, pale gravel | Snow-tipped firs, broken parapets and cairns | A courtyard keep inside the inward spiral. A waterfall can spill over the eastern margin beside the outer climb. |
| i04 · Emerald Stepping Stones | Emerald moss, jade ledges, turquoise crystal light | Short rope posts, hanging lanterns and crystal clusters | Raise the northern road onto a terrace reached by a rope viaduct. Keep the southern cave mouth visible against the lower terrace. |
| i05 · Windmill Switchback | Wheat gold, amber stubble, russet orchard soil | Hay sheaves, low orchard rows and fence corners | Keep the mill between the western hairpins. Angle the field rows across the straight runs so players can distinguish each leg at a glance. |
| i06 · Crescent Lagoon | Pale coral sand, lagoon teal, seagrass green | Low palm fans, tide pools, shell banks and driftwood | A lighthouse overlooking the inner cove, with water inside the crescent and surf on the outer rim. Leave the south-facing shoreline around the entrance open. |
| i07 · Skybridge Gauntlet | Wind-worn grey stone, cool blue chasm, faded ochre turf | Snapped standards, low crenellations and bridge anchor blocks | Paired cliffside forts flank the chasm. Span it with stone on both the outward crossing and the lower return to the western gate. |
| i08 · Sunken Bastion | Peat brown, drowned olive, dark teal water, warm torchlight | Half-submerged masonry, reed clumps, torch brackets and broken coping | Flood the outer bailey and surround the inner gate with a moat. Use a short causeway on the final approach and a quieter water surface under the flyer circuit. |
