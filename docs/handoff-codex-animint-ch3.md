## Registration and playback

All sources have 192 frames at 24 fps. The packer follows stationary landmarks outside each working mask, estimates a similarity transform per frame, corrects the median channel offset and adds the painted opening residual. The match table records the largest correction at the mask origin, the maximum per-channel shift and feather-weighted wrap and adjacent-step measurements for every matched take. Retained takes use their shipping settings. Discarded takes use a 1× diagnostic crop with a two-layout-pixel inward feather.

Across the 70 new shipping loops, the largest registration correction was **2.24 start-frame pixels** on i07 southwest blossom boughs. The largest median channel correction was **7/255**. The largest raw wrap was **2.64/255** on i04 shrine sapling foliage, compared with its adjacent-step p95 of 3.40/255. Nine retained takes use the packer’s extra half-second end blend. The largest adjacent-step p95 was 7.09/255 on i06 southwest palm fronds.

Large water, meadow and canopy crops generally use one atlas texel per layout pixel. Small features and suspended objects use up to two. The full and compact downloads use silent BT.709 H.264 video and lossless WebP masks. All masters are 832 pixels wide. i19 has the tallest atlas at **832 × 1488 full and 416 × 744 compact**. The largest mesh contains 11 tiles on i02, or 44 vertices. The renderer builds one indexed mesh from the descriptor and has no fixed tile-count ceiling.
