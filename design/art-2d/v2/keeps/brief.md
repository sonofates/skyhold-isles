# Keep and coin paintings

These sources were generated with the built-in image generator on flat magenta and passed through `design/art-2d/key.py`. The biome plans and halls are 512-pixel keyed originals packed as WebP. The arch textures are rectified crops of the three compact gatehouse paintings. The coin master lives in `design/art-2d/out/icon-gold.png`.

## Generation prompts

Each plan and hall used the same theme substitution. The colours and materials follow the map kit.

- **orchard** — honey limestone, blue slate roofs and golden wheat pennants.
- **tidal** — weathered marsh timbers on a salt-grayed stone plinth, green patinated copper roofs, reed ochre banners.
- **highlands** — snow-dusted gray granite and slate, icy blue flags and snow on wall caps.
- **terraces** — sunlit ochre sandstone ashlar, muted terracotta hipped roofs, forest green banners.
- **hollow** — moonlit dusky violet bluestone, deep midnight-blue slate and pale silver moon motifs; avoid pink paint.
- **ember** — dark graphite basalt blocks, blackened roof tiles, warm copper details and burnt-orange banners.
