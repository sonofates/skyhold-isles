# Gatehouse paintings

The shipped maps use the keyed WebPs in this directory. The built-in image generator painted the original sprites on solid magenta. Each grid was divided along its magenta gutters, keyed at source resolution with `design/art-2d/key.py::key_image`, trimmed to the silhouette, and saved as WebP with alpha. Keep the full resolution when replacing a source. The 3× bake samples these paintings directly.

The west-facing sheet defines the materials. Its east-facing counterpart is a horizontal reflection. The north, south and diagonal turnarounds carry the same architecture around the footprint. The two compact reference paintings were widened for the final facade revision. The orchard northwest reference came from the west sprite rotated to match the road, then repainted so the turrets stand upright. Sill registrations and tunnel masks are in [keeps.py](../../../../tools/map-compose/keeps.py).
