# Highland Citadel

The western meltwater runs under a packhorse bridge, drops into a plunge pool and falls a second time into the gorge. The snow shelf between the water and the spiral carries the western tower pads. Both the garrison hall and the flag on the west postern carry the gold fir from the intro. The watchfire in the eastern court holds split logs for the beacon lighting in the outro.

## Source paintings

The built-in ImageGen tool produced the paintings below. The compositor uses the finished WebPs from this directory.

| File | Generation brief and registration |
| --- | --- |
| `terrain-i03.webp` | Paint the old snowfield from its fixed overhead camera. Follow a narrow stream from the west edge over two granite shelves. Put a stone packhorse bridge above the first drop, a turquoise plunge pool between the falls and spray in the lower gorge. Keep the northeastern fissure and the central snowfield. On the fitted plate, repaint the pinched stones and spruce beside the plunge pool. Pull the left bank away from the two western pads. Raise the first plunge pool so the whitewater lands above the phone ability bar. |
| `fir-postern-e.webp` | Retexture the existing east-facing cave in violet slate, weathered beams and snow. Hang a teal pennon with a gold fir on the near post. Retain the dark aperture and the rock silhouette. The finish is registered to the two measured jambs on the original 404 × 243 canvas. |
| `landmark-fir-hall.webp` | Paint a compact granite great hall with a steep snow-striped slate roof, two lit lancet windows, fir banner, timber door, three stone steps and a square corner tower. Use a steep three-quarter view and isolate the entire footprint. |
| `landmark-fir-beacon.webp` | Set a cold iron fire basket full of split wood on a short round stone drum. Add two steps, a poker and a carved fir on the front block. Show the wood in the open basket from above. |
| `landmark-kindling-sled.webp` | Lash split billets and a small hatchet onto a low ash sledge. Turn its bowed runners toward the lower left and let the tow rope fall slack ahead of them. Give the sledge warm cut ends and cool metal runner strips. |
| `snow-spruces.webp` and `winter-rowan.webp` | Paint three spruce of different ages with scalloped snow on the blue-green boughs. In a separate painting, place a low rowan beside two snow-capped stones. Give it red berries, bare wind-bent stems and curled burgundy leaves. Both groups have transparent margins and visible feet. |
| `tex-frost-setts.webp` | Paint a whole military courtyard from overhead in tightly fitted granite setts with crisp mortar and small icy edge highlights. Four courses cut from the field form a continuous strip, with a transverse course at each join. The bake unrolls the strip along the spiral between the postern and keep. |

The black areas of `dressing-i03.webp` reserve the waterfalls, bridge, fissure and cliff lips from scatter. The shallow heel and toe impressions in `landmark-snow-steps.webp` follow the walk from the verge to the sled handle. The map and foregrounds carry all the new scenery at runtime.

Rebuild the four map sizes and both pairs of fronts with `/usr/bin/python3 -B tools/map-compose/rebake_all.py i03`.
