### Final-island road and floor polish

Each `tex-i43.webp`, `tex-i44.webp` and `tex-i45.webp` is a single 1846 × 852 road painting in layout coordinates. ImageGen painted over each approved 1× map, including all branches and junctions. The painted shoulders were registered to the existing spline contours and the adjoining material extended under each threshold. In `campaign.json` the three recipes select `roadPlate`. The compositor applies its existing gate taper, road mask and keep apron at every resolution.
