# Eclipse Skies island paintings

The built-in ImageGen tool supplied these original terrain plates, transparent scenery sheets, road materials, chart miniatures and story panels. The source kit lives in `design/art-2d/v2/biomes/eclipse`. The compositor lays the roads along the simulation paths and seats the painted gates at their thresholds. The two spans on i38 come from the bridge cell of its scenery sheet.

The polish pass used the existing terrain plates and east-facing gate sprites as ImageGen edit references. The three road materials are new paintings. In the bake, i37’s crossboards and i38’s chipped slate follow the lane tangent. On i39, a first swatch bake served as the reference for a second ImageGen paint-over. The final road plate contains both branches and the shared exit, with bare earth extended under each gate. The compositor clips it to the simulation spline. Only the flat interior accents of the i38 and i39 terrain edits were composited back onto the plates. The registered gate finishes use the original gate silhouette and opening, with a mirrored finish for the east entrance on i39.
