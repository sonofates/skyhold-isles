# Frostveil Archipelago · i28–i33

Painted with the built-in image generator. The terrain plates, cutouts and road materials live in `design/art-2d/v2/biomes/frostveil/`. Runtime bakes and story panels live in `public/art2d/` and `public/art2d/ui/`.

The three scenery atlases retain their transparent originals. The i28 generator placed its ice cave in the upper-right cell. The kit holds a cropped, east-facing version of each gate, which the compositor mirrors for west-facing entrances. The three road swatches are scaled to 416 px and their highlights reduced for phone readability. The wide terrain plates carry the large landmarks and rim architecture. The recipes add the smaller cutouts and road shoulders. Pinewhite Ridge also uses the compositor’s paired cart tracks.
