# New island paintings

The terrain plates, four-object scenery atlases, road materials and story panels use the built-in image generator. The first atlas object is the island landmark. The road compositor places it with the gates and reserves its footprint during plot placement.

## i25 · Galeglass Orrery

The two roads clasp the instrument like the open jaws of a caliper. Its keeper must catch the storm’s blue hour before the lens goes dark. [Biome kit](../v2/biomes/highlands/)

### Terrain

Original Skyhold Isles game environment terrain painting of a storm-swept highland charting lawn, soft lichen green over cool bluish shale, silver oats, lavender cloudbanks just beyond a broken dark slate rim at the extreme left, lower right and top. Grey spires at far left edge. Very wide panoramic 2.17:1 landscape, bird’s-eye near-orthographic view straight down. A single enormous flat usable plateau fills the whole central 84% of the frame including the entire top centre. Margin detail occupies ONLY outer 8%. Hand-painted storybook gouache, broad chunky silhouettes, delicate drybrush accents, overhead warm upper-left light. Keep the central ground softly mottled, continuous and low contrast. Full bleed, no horizon, buildings, roads, trails, gates, circles, plots, UI, text or borders.

### Scenery

Original Skyhold Isles game scenery atlas, a SINGLE HORIZONTAL ROW of exactly four separate fully isolated sprites on true transparent background, approximately 3:1 canvas. Each fits entirely inside its own equally wide quarter, generous clear gutters. In order from left: (1) a large brass armillary storm instrument with three open tilted circular rings and a suspended blue glass droplet over a squat stone plinth, (2) a forked bronze sight post with a blue vane, (3) a small dense clump of silver oat grass, (4) a fractured blue quartz sample on slate. Each object complete to its base, chunky readable miniature silhouettes from a high three-quarter top-down strategy-map viewpoint, hand-painted gouache and thin broken ink, soft upper-left light. Each centred in its column, no ground tile, no scenery connecting pieces, no letters, numbering, labels or border.

### Road

Square seamless road surface material for the Skyhold Isles painted strategy map. Straight down orthographic, continuous warm smoke-grey small triangular shale pavers with pale buff mica joints and buff worn chips. Two metres across. Small grouped shapes, gouache and drybrush, soft upper-left light, low contrast, tactile. Fill entire square. No verges, borders, perspective, people, letters or decoration.

### Intro

Wide cinematic 2:1 full bleed original Skyhold Isles storybook gouache painting. At the height of a dark squall a weather-reader in a saffron oilskin holds up a loose blue glass plumb bob; behind her the empty bronze rings of the great storm orrery point in three different directions. Wind lifts her charts, a white fork of lightning silhouettes the shale spires. Intimate low-angle medium shot. Painterly edges with restrained sepia contours, bold readable silhouettes, richly layered colour, no text, borders, lettering or UI.

### Outro

Wide cinematic 2:1 full bleed original Skyhold Isles storybook gouache painting. Sun breaks through on a high meadow. Seen through two immense concentric brass rings, a blue plumb bob catches a single sunbeam and throws a brilliant pin of light on the keeper’s open palm. Cloud shadows lift off a distant floating sanctuary. Quiet and precise, morning silver and honey. Painterly edges with restrained sepia contours, bold readable silhouettes, richly layered colour, no text, borders, lettering or UI.
