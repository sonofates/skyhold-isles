# Chapter two island paintings

The built-in image generator painted the terrain, scenery and story panels. The compositor adds the road and gates from the saved paths. The cliff and cavern masks reserve the architecture painted into the terrain.

## Cloudbell Cloister

The terrain, mask, and cut scenery are in [the i17 biome kit](../v2/biomes/cloister/). The two story paintings are in `public/art2d/ui/story-i17-{intro,outro}-1.webp`.

### Terrain

Original production environment painting for Skyhold Isles, a hand-painted storybook tower defense. Wide landscape 2.17:1. A bird's-eye almost orthographic view down onto a single huge flat high-altitude limestone meadow, the forgotten belfry gardens. Flat open buildable pale sage-green and warm straw turf fills the central 80% of the entire canvas. Asymmetrical rim of layered ivory limestone drops off into luminous blue and pale lavender cloudbanks at the far left and far right edges, with a few smaller distant floating crags. At bottom left in the margin, one broken ring of timeworn limestone columns with tiny muted copper bells, blue pennants and yellow wildflowers. Top right edge a small ruined arcade. Ground center is uninterrupted gently mottled thin grass over old limestone tile fragments, subtle warm ochre fern patches, local detail and a sense of scale, fine dry-brushed gouache strokes. Paint clear clean silhouettes, warm directional sunlight from upper left, restrained contrast, chunky shapes with thin painted dark accents and softly shaded sides. Fill the entire image. This is a clean terrain background beneath a game map: no paths or roads anywhere, no castles, towers, gates, build pads, figures, text, border, HUD or UI. No horizon, uniform orthographic view above the ground. Broad left and right access for a road. Landscape composition, full bleed.

## Lantern Glassworks

The terrain, mask, and cut scenery are in [the i18 biome kit](../v2/biomes/glassworks/). The two story paintings are in `public/art2d/ui/story-i18-{intro,outro}-1.webp`.

### Road

Square seamless production game terrain texture, top-down orthographic flat ground, fill entire square with worn blue-grey slate flagging in an underground glassworks. Broad uneven slabs of dusty desaturated blue-grey and smoky slate with warmer worn edges, darker petrol blue thin seams, sparse flecks of old aquamarine glass and copper grit. A continuous, well travelled work floor, medium value overall, rich but subdued. Thin gouache brush marks, occasional scored chisel lines, softly irregular shapes, small quiet areas, subtle upper left cool illumination and occasional amber reflection. No bright white stones, glowing veins, objects, grass, paths within the tile, vignette, borders or writing. Original hand-painted storybook game texture.

The published 768 px road material uses a gamma exponent of 0.70 on each color channel. The lighter slate keeps the return loop visible against the cavern floor on a phone.

Original Skyhold Isles gouache-painted orthographic tower defence game terrain, wide landscape EXACTLY 2.17:1. Underground glassblowers' cavern on a floating island. Vast broad uninterrupted flat dark muted petrol-blue slate floor with irregular natural seams and tiny mineral inclusions covers center 85%, traversable from left to right. Slightly top-down 3/4 camera facing north, no horizon. Along extreme top edge only, crystal cave walls with a few slender ceiling stalactites. At far LEFT and bottom left, luminous cyan-green glass spars rise in an old sunken quarry, two antique copper kiln chimneys glowing dull amber, a tiny abandoned tram tub, some chalky quartz boulders. At far RIGHT edge, a shallow crystalline crevasse and violet geode face. The central floor must be open and spacious to place towers and a road on top of later. Classic original illustrated storybook gouache, chunky painted solids, cool lilac mineral accents, dark warm ink edges, readable forms, subtle green and orange glancing light, low contrast open floor, high contrast characteristic cavern silhouette at edges. NO paths, roads, tracks crossing the middle, circles, symbols, characters, towers, buildings in center, text, border, UI. Actual clean playable map underlay, edge to edge, bird's eye, never a side view.
