# Cannon specialization source paintings

The built-in image generator supplied the five turret directions for each branch and a separate painting of the two workshops. The left and right workshop paintings were reduced to 512×512 and placed in the first cell of the corresponding lossless source atlas. The remaining cells retain the generated turret paintings at their native size.

## Registration

Run `/usr/bin/python3 tools/sprites/pack_specialization_turrets.py` from the checkout. In `cannons.json`, each foot is the center of the turntable's contact patch and each tip is the shell origin in the same source cell. Honeycomb launches from the center of its three openings. Bell Foundry launches from the middle of its bell mouth. The southeast composites supply the codex portraits.
