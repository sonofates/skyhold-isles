# Frost and Tesla specialization sheets

The built-in ImageGen tool generated four 1536×1024 magenta parts sheets. Each prompt consisted of the shared direction below followed by the branch paragraph. The lossless `*-atlas.webp` sources preserve the original pixels.

## Shared direction

Use case: illustration-story. A production cutout game sprite PARTS sheet on utterly flat solid #ff00ff magenta. Wide landscape 3:2 canvas. SIX equally sized invisible cells, THREE columns TWO rows; no outlines around cells, no labels. Orthographic 3/4 high side view looking down 28 degrees. World style: original storybook painted strategy game, warm beveled sandstone, gouache modeling, firm dark contour, crisp large simple shapes legible at 75 pixels, lit upper left. NO floor plane or outside cast shadow, horizon, border, text or detached particles. Each item entirely isolated with generous magenta gutters, centered in its own cell. TOP LEFT is a single complete BUILDING BASE on a round low honey-colored flagstone plinth seen as horizontal ellipse. TOP MIDDLE, TOP RIGHT, BOTTOM LEFT, BOTTOM MIDDLE each contain ONLY THE SAME detachable crown in four attack poses. BOTTOM RIGHT is EMPTY magenta. Only ONE building BASE in the whole sheet. The four crowns have matching scale, perspective and pivot. Crown plugs into the top middle of the base. No bases or stones below the crowns.

## snowbell

Snowbell Garden is the home of sky gardeners. BASE: squat warm limestone circular garden pavilion with a little arched marine-green door, two low rounded greenhouse wings ribbed with bronze and pale jade glass, little drifts of snow, top platform with a central empty brass socket. Keep the stone ring plainly visible to the sides and across the bottom. CROWNS: each is an upright botanical cluster of THREE snowdrop flowers on dark teal arcing leaves and green stalks springing from a small brass bulb at bottom center. Oversized ivory bells of thick porcelain, blue-green inner petals, ice blue stamens. In top middle flowers hang quietly together; in top right stalks draw the blooms inward to charge; in bottom left they fan broadly out and open revealing icy starlit hearts for release; in bottom middle petals relax and bow again. Bold hanging petal silhouettes and dark blue shading. Garden, hand-crafted, magical and cozy.

## rimeglass

Rimeglass Needle is a mountain glass cutter's cold observatory. BASE: narrow tall tapering deep petrol-blue slate and warm sandstone watchtower, recessed golden lit arched slit, sloping shoulders clad in teal tiles with two ice-coated buttresses, and a dark brass pronged socket clearly centered at the very top to receive the needle. Broad plain sand-colored circular foundation ring visibly projects at the sides/bottom. CROWNS: ONE long slim spearpoint of cut pale cyan glass, held upright in a tiny brass ferrule at its bottom center with two very short angled dark-teal flanking slivers. Top middle is idle, facets glint; top right is charging, deepening blue with a bright seam creeping up its core; bottom left releases, a brilliant white star at the upper tip and three sharp little frost slivers flaring out beside the tip; bottom middle has fading silver streaks and settles. Clean angular triangular silhouette, crystalline and precise. No fog, halos or big magical clouds.

## relay

Relay Grove is an arborist's sky-lightning watchtower. BASE: chunky short round building woven of dark carved oak trunks over warm golden sandstone, little arched door, a strip of green-glazed roof tiles and a bright copper conductor loop hugging the wood; top one empty bronze socket. Base plinth is a broad level ring of tan beveled flagstones as wide as bottom of base. CROWN: one sculptural copper tree with three branching dark bronze boughs, each ending in a clearly separated round hard turquoise glass lantern, few bold trefoil leaves of oxidized green copper and a single copper foot bottom center. Tree reads like a wide candelabra. Top middle paint the boughs still; top right a bright charge gathering inside the three lanterns; bottom left paint two clear ice-blue arcs of lightning bridging all three turquoise spheres like a crown, center sphere sparks as the lightning leaps; bottom middle one last faint arc as the glass settles. Delicate details subordinated to bold silhouettes. Branches not a black silhouette; strong warm outline and illuminated volume.

## stormheart

Stormheart Coil is a storm-bell generator with a powerful heartbeat. BASE: compact hefty cylindrical warm sandstone engine house, midnight blue roof bands and two russet copper lightning cables curving down the flanks, one small glowing warm amber arched door, broad low honey-sandstone elliptical plinth, top one circular empty socket. CROWN: a bold faceted cobalt thunderstone suspended BETWEEN two heavy concentric COPPER GYROSCOPE HOOPS forming a distinctive round open armillary globe, mounted on a short foot bottom center; visible reddish-copper band in front and dark band behind the gem. Top middle hoops mildly tilted so the blue crystal shows, dormant. Top right hoops rotate and build electric-blue light at the stone. Bottom left hoops snap into perpendicular planes with a brilliant white-blue FOUR POINT STAR in the very center of the cobalt heart where lightning is released, faint forked sparks on inner rims. Bottom middle contracting spark, hoops return nearly to resting angle. Do not draw any frame larger than its own cell. Chunky, bold copper and sapphire shapes, no metal spires, no icy needles.

## Packing and registration

Run `/usr/bin/python3 tools/sprites/pack_specialization_attacks.py` from the checkout. The packer uses the project's `design/art-2d/key.py` on each cell. The socket, foot pivots and release point in `frost.json` and `tesla.json` use 512×512 cell-local coordinates. The socket belongs to the body. Each foot belongs to its corresponding crown and the release point belongs to the open crown in the lower-left cell.

Every sheet has eight full-body frames at 12 fps. Frame zero supplies the static map sprite, frame three releases the effect, and frame seven returns to the resting painting. The packer registers the same building on every frame, checks the stone ring and writes the release point into the descriptor in final frame pixels. The codex portrait is a larger export of the resting painting.
